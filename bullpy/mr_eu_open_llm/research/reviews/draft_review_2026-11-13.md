# Independent review of draft v2, 2026-11-13

Scope: everything stated as fact or result in `research/paper/v2/` (abstract, introduction, results, methods, supplementary_methods, supplementary_note1, figure_legends, references, submission/*). discussion.md and outline.md were skipped as instructed. PENDING slots for A2, A4, A5, A7 Stage 1, A8 and A9 runs were not treated as defects. No draft, preregistration, script or data file was edited.

File references below are relative to `research/paper/v2/` unless they start with `research/`, `scripts/` or `results/`.

How it was checked. Every number in the drafts was compared with `research/RESULTS_REGISTRY.md` and with the output file the registry names. I recomputed from the files with `/opt/anaconda3/bin/python` for: A1 (`rotation_summary.json`, and the 30 pulled eval files under `results/baseline/eu_emotions/`), A3 (`face_input_validity_summary.json`), the natural-clip scores and their route recovery (`same_clip_family_model_*.json`, `same_clip_family_route_pattern_*within_category.json`), A4 ceilings, energy shares and recovery (`a4_human_ceiling.json`, `a4_recovery.json`, `a4_recovery_run3.json`), A9 target, ceiling and recovery (`a9_human_target.json`, `a9_human_ceiling.json`, `a9_recovery*.json`), cue spectra and the Proposition 2 and Theorem 1 checks (`cfa_identifiability.json`), delivery and vision gates, A7 Stage 0, the A2 skewed-gain simulation, the prompt audit, the weak-label means and the literature table counts. Decision rules were read against `PREREGISTRATION.md`, `PREREG_A2/A3/A4/A5/A7/A8/A9_DRAFT.md` and `DECISIONS.md`. Freeze dates were checked with `git tag` and `git log`. Citations were checked against `references.md`, `citation_check.md` and `LIT_CHECK_2026-11-07.md`, and existence was checked through the arXiv, Crossref and DataCite APIs.

## Summary

Most numbers check out. Every A1, A3, natural-clip, A4-design, A7 Stage 0, cue-spectrum and A9 value I recomputed matches its file at the stated rounding. Every reference resolves to a real record whose title and authors match, so nothing looks fabricated.

The problems are in claims and in internal consistency:

1. **Theory claim (High).** The abstract and cover letter say the paper proves that natural-item agreement "cannot settle" cue use "because natural cues are correlated". That misstates Theorem 1, which rests on freedom off the natural support, not on correlation. It also contradicts the paper's own Proposition 3 and Supplementary Note 1, Section 6.
2. **Preregistration record (High).** `submission/preregistration_links.md` lists A7 as not preregistered and has no A8 or A9 row. The journal will check this file.
3. **A misplaced number (Medium).** In the natural-clip recovery paragraph, the half-shared power of .83 reads as the different-cues observer's size.
4. **Undisclosed data structure (Medium).** Snoek's basic and conversational sets share AU configurations within each culture. This is not disclosed, and it explains the identical spectrum rows in two tables.
5. **Mislabelled figure panel (Medium).** Figure 3c labels a matrix-error value as a change in natural-item outputs.
6. **Missing deviations (Medium).** The 2026-11-12 pre-data deviations of A4, A5 and A9 are not in Methods.
7. **Stale text (Medium).** Several passages in Results 6b, Supplementary Methods S10 and the submission files no longer match what has been frozen or run.

A hostile reviewer would also go for the confirmatory natural-clip alignment. For Gemma 4 and Molmo2 it rests on an input that failed the paper's own delivery gate. Molmo2's score of .543 sits beside chance accuracy, a modal "None of the above" on 58% of A1 real cells, and a token-swap flip rate of 0.
