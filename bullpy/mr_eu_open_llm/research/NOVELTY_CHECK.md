# Novelty check: VLMs against randomised human cue effects

**Run:** 2026-11-04. Claim tested: no one has tested vision-language models against randomised human cue effects on faces.
**Result:** No counterexample found. This is absence of evidence from the searches below, not proof.

## Searched

- arXiv API, 12 queries (action units, FACS, reverse correlation, classification images, Bubbles, psychophysics, synthetic faces, generative face grammar, counterfactual, diagnostic). Zero hits: reverse correlation with VLM, GPT or language-model terms; Bubbles with the same; generative face grammar.
- Web search, extended: VLM reverse correlation; GPT, Gemini and Claude on manipulated AUs; psychophysics, Bubbles and occlusion on multimodal LLMs; morphs and composites in PsyArXiv-style work; VSS, CCN and CogSci 2026 abstracts; Jack lab work.
- Crossref keyword search from 2024.
- OpenAlex: all 29 works by R. E. Jack since January 2024. None uses a VLM or LLM as a perceiver.

## Nearest work, and why it is not a counterexample

| Paper | What it does | Why not |
|---|---|---|
| Kramer 2025, J Nonverbal Behavior | ChatGPT labels emotions and codes AUs on photographs | No randomised cues |
| Nelson et al. 2025, npj Digital Medicine | GPT-4o, Gemini, Claude on NimStim | Accuracy on posed photographs |
| Strachan et al. | GPT-4o on RMET, with inversion | Confusion, not cues |
| EmoNet-Face 2025 | Human and VLM annotation of synthetic faces | Agreement, no cue randomisation |
| Fujii and Minato 2026, Sci Reports | LLMs drive a robot's expressions; humans rate them | LLM generates, does not perceive. Jack is cited, not an author |
| PLOS ONE 2023, GAN with reverse correlation | AU-driven GAN reverse correlation in humans | Humans only |
| VisAULa, TAG, ActFER and similar | VLMs trained or prompted to use AUs | Engineering; no human cue data |
