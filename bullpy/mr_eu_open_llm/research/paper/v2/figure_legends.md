# Figure legends

**Figure 1 | Audit of the earlier task.** **a**, Position of the acted label in the six-option list of the v1 prompt. The target was first on every item, so accuracy equals the share of first-option choices. **b**, Option sets. Each row is an acted label and each filled cell a label offered on its items; None of the above, offered on every item, is not shown. Each label had one option set, so the options identify the category without the image. Excitement is the workbook's spelling on Kind items and is kept as written. **c**, Delivery. Median image tokens across the face, over the 243 clips, per input route and model. Dashed line, the gate of 8 tokens. Filled markers pass all four delivery gates; open markers fail at least one. On Gemma 4 the composite grid (`legacy_full`) leaves about 1 token across the face. **d**, Weak labels. Mean share of face-only human observers choosing the acted label, per label, from the item norms. Orange, the three flagged weak labels (Kind, Unfriendly, Jealous). Dashed line, chance at 1/6. Inputs: `prompt_confound_audit.json`, `input_delivery_{gemma4,qwen3vl,molmo2}.json`, `data/eu_emotions_human_entropy.json`. No model response is plotted.

**Figure 3 | Behaviour on natural items cannot settle cue use.** **a**, Proposition 2. Observer S responds to cue z1 only and observer T to cue z2 only. When the two cues correlate with rho on natural clips, the two outputs correlate with rho, but their cue effects are orthogonal, so CFA is 0. **b**, The same case computed: output correlation and CFA between S and T at rho of .5, .9 and .99. Behaviour converges while CFA stays at 0. **c**, Theorem 1 computed. Systems built to match a fixed system on every natural item reach any requested CFA with it; the largest change in any natural-item output is shown. Grey line, identity. **d**, Smallest eigenvalue of the cue correlation matrix per cue table, log scale. Blue, detector cues on the 243 EU-Emotion clips (LibreFace, OpenFace 3.0). Orange and green, the randomised Snoek designs with action units as coded and with left and right versions merged. Dashed line, 1, the value for uncorrelated cues. By Proposition 3, 1 / lambda_min bounds how far behavioural agreement can understate a difference in cue effects. **e**, Condition number of the same matrices. Natural clips are far less well conditioned than the randomised designs, and most of the Snoek correlation comes from bilateral pairs. Input: `research/reproduction/cfa_identifiability.json`; theory in `research/theory/causal_feature_alignment.md`.
